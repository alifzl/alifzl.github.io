My approach

I only fix what
I can measure

I measure what a problem costs before proposing anything. If the savings won't cover the cost of fixing it, I say so and stop there.

Baseline

I establish where the problem starts, what it affects and what it costs. You get the number and the method behind it.

Root cause

I identify what actually drives the problem — architecture, workload, cost, data quality or process. Until the cause is clear, implementation is guesswork.

Proof

I measure the change on your data, in your environment. The result, the method and the code stay with you.

The entry point

Start with one defined problem
and a measurable baseline

Begin with a focused assessment of one costly problem. Expand only if the findings justify it.
